---
name: find-a-project
description: Use when Mishi wants something to build, says she's bored or stuck, or mentions a recurring annoyance, goal, routine or something she tracks by hand. Interviews her to find small, worthwhile things to build.
---
1. Read `~/.claude/IDEAS.md` if it exists, so you don't re-suggest what's there.
2. Interview her one question at a time about her days and weeks: routines, recurring annoyances, things she tracks in her head or on paper, goals she keeps not getting to, things she does by hand every week. Follow up on anything with energy in it.
3. After 5–10 questions, propose 3 candidates. For each: the problem in her words, the lightest solution (no app, artifact, web, Mac or iPhone) and why, a rough effort (minutes, hours, days), and what her life looks like once it exists.
4. Show the candidates as an artifact (the show-me skill).
5. Add the ones she likes to IDEAS.md (one line each, with the date).
6. Offer to start the best one with grill-me. For an artifact-sized idea, the grill can be 5 questions.
