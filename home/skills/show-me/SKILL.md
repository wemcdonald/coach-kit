---
name: show-me
description: Use when presenting a plan, spec, options, screenshots, progress or a summary to Mishi, or when she says "show me".
---
Publish a well-designed Claude artifact instead of a long chat message.
- Lead with the one thing she needs to know or decide.
- Use headings, short paragraphs, and images or screenshots where they help. End with a clear "What I need from you" section.
- One artifact per topic per project (for example, "Habit app: spec"). Update that one instead of making new ones, and keep its link in PROGRESS.md.
- Then ask for her reactions in chat. Comments on artifacts need a Team plan, and she's on her own account, so chat is where her feedback comes in.
- Never put passwords, keys or other people's private information in an artifact.
